ClinicVox Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Publisher: isuxtech

Demo release. This version of ClinicVox is a demonstration app. It uses sample data only, has no user accounts on a server, and does not send your information to isuxtech.

1. Who we are

ClinicVox ("the app") is published by isuxtech ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at gabbyreads2020@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

Voice input. When you use voice triage, the app uses your device's built-in Android speech recognition service to turn your speech into text. On many devices this service is provided by Google, which may process the audio on its servers to produce the transcript. That processing is governed by the speech service provider's own privacy policy (for Google, see policies.google.com/privacy). ClinicVox does not record, save or upload the audio itself.

Symptoms and profile details you type or say. Information you enter, such as your name, symptoms or appointment choices, is held only in the app's memory while you use it. It is not saved on your device and is not sent to us. It is cleared when the app is closed.

Approximate location. If you allow it, the app reads your device's approximate (not precise) location when you open the appointment booking screen, to show how far each clinic is and list the nearest first. The distance is calculated on your device. Your location is not stored and is not sent to us or anyone else. If you don't grant the permission, clinics are listed without distances.

Sign-in details. Sign-in screens in this demo (including the clinician portal) do not check against a real account server, and the details you enter are not stored or sent anywhere.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store your health information, audio recordings or transcripts.
  • We do not collect or share your location. It is only used on your device, as described above.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you are actively using voice triage, to convert speech to text.
  • Approximate location (optional): used only to show distances to nearby clinics.
  • Notifications: requested so the app can show appointment reminders. No information is sent off your device to do this.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings. The app will still work, with typed input instead of voice.

5. Medical disclaimer

ClinicVox is a demonstration app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Triage results, clinicians and appointments shown in the app are samples. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services straight away.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not store or send your information, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Closing or uninstalling the app removes everything you entered.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of ClinicVox collects or stores personal or health information, for example by connecting to a real clinic service, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

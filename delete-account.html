Delete your ClinicVox account and data

App: ClinicVox · Developer: isuxtech

This page explains how to delete a ClinicVox account (patient or clinician) and the data linked to it.

How to delete your account

  1. On your Android device, open Settings → Apps → ClinicVox.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  3. That's it. Your account and everything you entered are deleted straight away.

If you'd like us to confirm, or have any questions, email gabbyreads2020@gmail.com with the subject "ClinicVox account deletion". We will reply within 30 days.

Why this is all you need to do

The current version of ClinicVox is a demonstration app. Accounts you create in it, and the details you enter (name, email, password, symptoms, appointments), are held only in the app's memory on your device. They are never sent to or stored on isuxtech servers, so there is no copy for us to delete.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all account details, profile information, symptoms, triage answers and appointment choices.
  • Kept: nothing. isuxtech does not keep any of your data, and there is no extra retention period.

Voice input is turned into text by your device's speech recognition service (often provided by Google), which handles that audio under its own privacy policy. ClinicVox itself does not save audio.

See also the ClinicVox Privacy Policy.
